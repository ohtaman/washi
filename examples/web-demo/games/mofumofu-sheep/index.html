🐑 How to Play Guide

1. Tap a tile to place a fence

Block wolf paths with wooden fences so wolves cannot reach any sheep. Tap again to remove.

🍓
2. Build a spacious pasture to achieve the target score

While keeping wolves out, the total score of all tiles reachable by sheep must reach or exceed the target score to clear.

🕳️
3. Stone Bridges 🌉 and Mud Pits 🕳️

Stone bridges 🌉 are walkable, but fences cannot be built on them. Mud pits 🕳️ (-5 pt) deduct score if inside your pasture, but placing a wooden fence directly on a pit fills it in completely!
