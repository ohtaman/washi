🔰 Quick Guide: 4-Step Walkthrough for AI University Timetabling (UCTP)
STEP 1 Master Data (Faculty, Rooms, Courses)

Benchmark faculty (10), classrooms/labs (7), and courses (25) are pre-loaded. Inspect or import your own CSVs in the 📚 Master Data tab.

STEP 2 Configure Rules & Consult AI

Configure academic constraints such as instructor availability, consecutive lab periods, and PC room requirements in the ⚙️ Rules tab or chat.

STEP 3 Auto-Schedule Timetable (HiGHS Optimization)

When ready, click "⚡ Auto-Schedule Timetable". WaSHI (HiGHS) optimizes all 4 cohorts, faculty, and rooms simultaneously in seconds.

STEP 4 Fine-Tuning (Drag & Drop, AI Chat)

Drag and drop sessions on the board to adjust, or chat naturally with AI Copilot to inject custom pinning rules. Infeasible conflicts are auto-diagnosed with WaSHI IIS.
